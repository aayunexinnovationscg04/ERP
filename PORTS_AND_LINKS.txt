WHY 127.0.0.1 vs 31.42.125.144
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Public traffic now enters ONLY on 443 (Caddy) and 80 (device + ACME). Caddy
  proxies the portal subdomains -> nginx :8090 -> gunicorn :8000. nginx and
  gunicorn are internal engine parts; the public never hits 8090/8000 directly.
  This is standard, secure architecture and needs no extra provider-firewall ports.
