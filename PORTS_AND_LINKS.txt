WHY 127.0.0.1 vs 31.42.125.144
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  31.42.125.144:PORT = public front doors you actually use (nginx 8090/8091, Caddy 80/443).
  127.0.0.1:PORT     = internal engine parts (gunicorn, Postgres) that only nginx
                       talks to. The backend IS public - through nginx - not on its
                       own exposed port. This is standard, secure architecture.


REACHABILITY CAVEAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ports 8090/8091 work on the server and the OS firewall is open, but the CLOUD
  PROVIDER firewall may only allow 80/443/22 from the internet. If the ERP links
  don't open from your browser, either open 8090/8091 in the provider panel, OR
  move the ERPs onto the domain via 443 (e.g. owner.aayunexinnovations.com) so no
  extra ports are needed.
