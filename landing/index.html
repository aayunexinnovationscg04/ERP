What it does

Built for real trucks on 2G

Firmware-compatible ingest, a derivation engine that turns raw telemetry into trips and alerts, and a carrier-NAT command queue — all grounded in real device data.

Live GPS tracking

Every device POST lands as telemetry; latest position and full route history on the map.

Geofencing

Draw allowed/restricted zones; enter/exit events and breach alerts computed server-side.

Smart alerts

Overspeed, geofence breach, tamper (cap opened), device offline — debounced, not noisy.

Role-based access

Multi-tenant by company, configurable per-role tabs, and per-member overrides.

Device command queue

Queue actions for the ESP32; delivered exactly-once in the device's next POST reply.

Secured by default

HTTPS everywhere, JWT auth, throttled logins, firewalled server, secrets out of git.
